How a war on NATO's eastern flank would begin

Russia has started three wars in eighteen years, and each was preceded by a recognisable set of observable indicators. This estimate plots that history onto the ground where it happened, catalogues every significant incident on NATO territory over the last twelve months, and asks the only question that matters for the next twenty four: which of those indicators are present today, and which are missing.
